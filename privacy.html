Privacy Policy

Last updated

The (“the application”) is operated by to record and show the safety training status of its personnel. This policy explains what information the application handles and how it is used.

Who can use the application

Only people whose Google account email has been registered by an administrator can sign in. Anyone else who signs in is shown a “Not registered” message and can see no data.

Information we receive from Google

When you sign in with Google, the application receives your email address, your name and your Google account identifier. These are used only to:

  • confirm that your email is registered and decide what you are allowed to see (viewer, manager or administrator);
  • keep you signed in; and
  • record which administrator last changed a training record.

The application does not access your Gmail, contacts, files, calendar or any other Google data.

Personnel and training information

Administrators enter information about personnel whose training is tracked: name, employee number, photo, position, job function, department, section, employer, work email and phone (optional), date joined, and training records (course, completion date, expiry date, certificate number, provider and remarks). This information is used only to show training status and compliance to registered users.

Who can see the information

  • Registered viewers can view individual personnel training pages.
  • Managers can also view the overall compliance report.
  • Administrators can add, change and remove information and manage access.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

Where the information is stored

The web pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The data, photos and sign-in are handled by Supabase, a cloud database service, with access restricted by database security rules so that only registered users can read data and only administrators can change it. Sign-in is provided by Google. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Cookies and local storage

The application stores a sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies or analytics.

How long information is kept

Training records are kept for as long as they are needed for safety training and compliance purposes. Access for a registered user is removed when an administrator suspends or deletes it. Personnel who leave can be marked inactive or deleted by an administrator.

Your choices and requests

You can ask to see, correct or delete information held about you by contacting the Safety Training Team at . You can stop using the application at any time and can remove its access from your Google account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
